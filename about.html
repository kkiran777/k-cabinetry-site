About

Earning Trust Through Craftsmanship

K Cabinetry is built on decades of experience delivering high-quality cabinets, countertops, and vanities for residential and commercial projects across North America.

Our Story

With production capabilities in the United States and Europe, we combine modern manufacturing technology with traditional attention to detail. Our teams focus on consistent quality, reliable schedules, and finishes that meet the expectations of developers, contractors, and designers.

Whether the project calls for classic shaker profiles, contemporary frameless construction, or coordinated surfaces, we approach each order with the same commitment to precision.

Operational Strength

Strategic facilities and modern equipment allow us to support both large multi-unit orders and detailed custom work while maintaining finish consistency and delivery reliability.
